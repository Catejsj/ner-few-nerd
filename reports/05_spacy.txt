[2] TOKENIZATION ALIGNMENT
    If we let spaCy tokenize the raw sentence text, it produces a
    different token sequence from Few-NERD's in 922 of 3,000 sentences (30.7%).
    Every one of those would misalign the labels.
    We therefore pass the gold tokens in directly with Doc(words=...),
    which turns the tokenizer off. Alignment is then exact by
    construction, and the comparison measures NER, not tokenization.

    'exact hits' is how many of those exactly match a gold Few-NERD span.

    low purity means the mapping is a compromise, not a translation.

    Numeric types (CARDINAL, DATE, MONEY, ORDINAL, PERCENT, QUANTITY, TIME) are dropped:
    Few-NERD has no type for dates, money or counts, so predicting them
    can only produce false positives. Dropping them is the mapping
    decision that most helps spaCy's score, and we state it openly.
